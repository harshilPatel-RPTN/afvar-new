# Earth textures

Used by the 3D earth in the /platform closing block (`src/modules/platform/components/closing/EarthScene.tsx`).

- `earth_day_4096.jpg` (Blue Marble), `earth_night_4096.jpg` (Black Marble city lights): NASA imagery (public domain), as prepared in the three.js examples (`examples/textures/planets`, r170, MIT).
- `moon_1024.jpg`: NASA moon albedo imagery (public domain), as prepared in the three.js examples (`examples/textures/planets`, MIT).
