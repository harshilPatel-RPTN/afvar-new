# AFVAR — Overview Reel Voice-Over Brief

**Project:** 80-second product reel for the AFVAR website
**Languages:** English · हिन्दी (Hindi) — one artist per language
**Length:** 1:20 (80.43 s), 10 lines, recorded to picture
**Guide videos:** `overview.en.mp4` / `overview.hi.mp4` (provided separately) — a synthetic guide voice at the exact timings. Match the *timing*, not the guide's delivery.

---

## 1. What AFVAR is (for context)

AFVAR is an AI assistant for traders. It follows commodities, forex and crypto, explains *why* markets move with the source behind every number, and helps traders review their own decisions.

It **never places trades and never gives investment advice.** The voice must never sound like it is selling a trading opportunity.

## 2. Tone

- **Calm, warm, quietly confident.** Picture a senior trader explaining something to a colleague over coffee. This is not an advert announcer.
- **Unhurried.** Pauses carry as much weight as words. Each line has room to breathe — use it.
- **Plain and honest.** No hype, no excitement spikes, no "trading-guru" energy. Avoid any upward "salesy" inflection at the ends of lines.
- **Line 3 ("…the honest answer is: wait")** — let *wait* land softly, with a small pause before it.
- **Line 9 ("Follow. Understand. Review.")** — three separate beats, about one second apart. Three calm statements; don't build them into a crescendo.
- **Line 10** — the sign-off. Slightly warmer and slower. *"Analysis, never advice"* should be clear and grounded, not rushed.

**References:** think Apple product films, or a good documentary narrator. Natural, intimate, close-mic.

> **The Hindi script is a draft.** A native speaker on the AFVAR side should approve them before the session. Small wording changes are fine as long as the meaning holds and each line still fits its max length. Don't add claims, numbers or promises.

## 5. Must not

- Say *buy*, *sell*, *profit*, *guaranteed* or anything that sounds like a trading tip. The script is deliberately free of these, so please don't ad-lib.
- Add music, sound effects, or reverb.
- Read the on-screen text that isn't in the script. Symbols like XAUUSD, labels and captions are not read aloud.

## 6. Delivery specs

- **Format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono
- **One full-length file per language, recorded to picture,** starting exactly at 00:00 of the guide video and running the full 80.43 s (silence between lines).
  Name: `AFVAR-reel-VO-en.wav`, `AFVAR-reel-VO-hi.wav`
- **Levels:** about −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Light cleanup only: de-noise, de-click and gentle compression. No heavy processing.
- **Also send:** the dry (unprocessed) version, 5 s of room tone, and 2 alternate takes of lines 1, 3 and 10 as separate files (`…-alt-L01-a.wav` etc.)
- **Room:** treated booth or a very quiet room, close-mic, no audible room reflections.

## 7. After delivery

AFVAR's team will mux the voice onto the video, so artists don't need any video software. They will also generate subtitles in both languages from the final timings. Pickups, if any, will reference this cue sheet's line numbers.
